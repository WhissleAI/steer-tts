# SteerTTS for Android: privacy policy

*Effective 25 October 2026. Publisher: Whissle.*

SteerTTS is a text-to-speech app that runs entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app reads, why,
and what happens to it.

## The short version

SteerTTS does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no
servers. Speech is generated on your phone.

## What the app reads, and why

| What | When | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Text you type or select** | When you ask the app to speak | To speak it | Nowhere: it is spoken on the phone and not kept |
| **Notification text** (sender and message) | Only if you turn on message readouts or incoming calls and grant notification access | To read new messages from the apps you choose, and to say who is calling | Nowhere: read, spoken and discarded on the phone |
| **Calendar events** (title, time, place of upcoming events) | Only if you turn on meeting reminders and grant calendar access | To announce a meeting a few minutes before it starts | Nowhere: read on the phone; the app keeps only a list of reminders already given, on the phone |
| **Health data from Health Connect** (last night's sleep, steps, resting heart rate) | Only if you connect Health Connect for the daily briefing | To say them in your briefing | Nowhere: read on the phone when the briefing plays, spoken and discarded; nothing is stored or written back |
| **Documents you open** (PDF, EPUB, Word, web page, text) | When you open or share a file with the app | To read it aloud | The extracted text is kept on your phone in your reading list until you remove it |
| **A link you share with the app** | Only when you share a link to "Read with SteerTTS" | To fetch that page and read its article aloud | The app downloads the page from the website you chose, like a browser would; nothing is sent to Whissle |

Your settings (voice, style, speed, which apps may be read) are stored on your phone only.

## Permissions

- **Notification access** (optional): message readouts and caller announcements. You grant it in Android's settings
  and can revoke it there at any time.
- **Calendar** (optional): meeting reminders and the briefing's look at your day.
- **Health Connect: sleep, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate** (optional, read only): the daily briefing. You can
  change or revoke it in Health Connect at any time.
- **Notifications**: the player notification with its play, pause and skip controls.
- **Alarms & reminders** (optional): on-time meeting reminders; without it they may be a little late.
- **Internet**: only to open a link you share with the app.
- **Run at startup**: to keep meeting reminders and the daily briefing scheduled after the phone restarts.

## Children

SteerTTS is not directed at children under 13.
