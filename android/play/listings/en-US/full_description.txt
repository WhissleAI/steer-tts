SteerTTS gives your phone an expressive voice that runs entirely on the device. No account, no internet connection, nothing sent anywhere: every word is made right on your phone.

LISTEN TO ANYTHING
• PDFs, EPUB e-books, Word documents, web pages and plain text, read aloud with a real player: pause, skip by sentence, speed from 0.8× to 2×, and the sentence being read highlighted as it goes.
• Controls on the lock screen and in the notification; your place in every document is remembered.
• Share an article or a file to SteerTTS from any app, or select text and choose "Read with SteerTTS".

SAY IT YOUR WAY
• One tap says the same words happy, whispered, excited or sad.
• Inline markup changes delivery mid-sentence: a whisper, stress on one word, a pause.
• 200 named voices with previews and favourites, eight styles from Calm to Whisper, a speed slider and simple controls for pitch, expressiveness, loudness and endings (with twelve fine controls behind Advanced).
• Reads the way people write: times, dates, phone numbers, prices, temperatures, titles such as Dr. and Mrs., and chapter numbers.

IT SPEAKS UP FOR YOU
• Message readouts: new messages from the apps you choose, read aloud as they arrive. By default only with headphones or in the car, never during a call or in Do Not Disturb.
• Incoming calls: hear who is calling while the phone rings.
• Meeting reminders: your next event announced a few minutes before it starts, including where it happens.
• Daily briefing: every morning, or whenever you ask, your day ahead plus last night's sleep, your steps and resting heart rate from Health Connect.

IT ADAPTS TO THE MOMENT
Alerts come out faster and louder, questions rise at the end, and late at night it speaks softer. You can switch this off.

YOUR PHONE'S VOICE
Set SteerTTS as Android's text-to-speech engine and other apps on your phone speak with it too.

FAST AND SMALL
• First sound in under 60 milliseconds on a single CPU core (Pixel 10).
• Renders speech more than 60 times faster than real time.
• The whole app, voice model included, is about 13 MB.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Notification text, calendar events, health data and your documents are read on the phone, spoken, and never sent anywhere. No account, no ads, no analytics. The app uses the internet only to open a link you share with it.

SteerTTS speaks English. Made by Whissle.
